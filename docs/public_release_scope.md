# Public release scope

## Included

- compact investor-flow feature construction;
- general structural-regime logic;
- expanding-window ridge forecast example;
- persistence benchmark and OOS metrics;
- sector utilities;
- synthetic data generator output;
- unit tests and CI configuration.

## Intentionally excluded

- Bloomberg / QuantWise / other licensed-data adapters and exact field mappings;
- source workbooks or cached vendor values;
- private code-manifest, audit, attestation and release-evidence trees;
- exact operational thresholds and private model registries;
- live forecast snapshots, private results and decision rules;
- Windows/Excel automation and local filesystem configuration.

## Why

The objective of this repository is to make the research process inspectable without publishing licensed data or unnecessary operational/IP detail. It is suitable as a recruiting / research-engineering portfolio artifact rather than as a public production system.
